# Code of Conduct

PocketRig follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and don't harass, insult or publish others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainer, [@knezzz](https://github.com/knezzz): open the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Code of Conduct". Only the maintainer can read it, and reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban the people who made them, temporarily or permanently, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
